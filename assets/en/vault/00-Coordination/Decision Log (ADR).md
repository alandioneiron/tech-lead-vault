## ADR-0001 - Adopt the tech-lead-vault method
- **Date:** {{DATE}}
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Context:** AI sessions start without memory; status and knowledge drift apart.
- **Decision:** the vault is the memory, GitHub issues/PRs/releases are the workflow, and every session ends with a handoff.
- **Consequences:** more documentation discipline per task; faster, safer session starts. See [[Definition of Done]].
